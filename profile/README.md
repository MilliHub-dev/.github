## About Millihub

Millihub is a technology foundation and studio helping founders, startups, and companies turn ambitious ideas into real-world solutions.

We operate at the intersection of software engineering, design, research, and ecosystem building. Our mission is to advance technology innovation across Nigeria and beyond by creating impactful products, supporting digital transformation, and strengthening the African tech ecosystem.

## What we do

- Software development and product engineering
- Artificial intelligence and emerging technology solutions
- Blockchain and digital infrastructure projects
- Web and mobile product design and development
- Research, simulation, and innovation strategy
- Drone and aerospace technology exploration
- Ecosystem support for founders and growing businesses

## Current focus

Millihub is actively supporting the growth of sustainable technology adoption and digital infrastructure in Nigeria, including work around:

- Electric mobility and clean transport innovation
- Digital product development for startups and enterprises
- Strategic collaboration across technology, mobility, and infrastructure sectors

## Mission

To help businesses and communities build with technology that is practical, scalable, and future-ready.

## Vision

To become a leading technology foundation for innovation, entrepreneurship, and digital growth across Africa.

## Core values

- Practical execution

- Long-term ecosystem growth

## Our approach

We combine technical execution with research and strategic insight to turn complex challenges into scalable solutions.

Whether we are building products, advising founders, or driving innovation partnerships, our work is guided by measurable impact and long-term sustainability.
